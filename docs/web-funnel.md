# The web funnel

The record of the web arc. The desktop is the visual reference, not the assignment: the goal
is the smallest fast-loading page on which a stranger understands why SoundGraph is
interesting, hears something, gets one golden moment, and has an obvious path to play,
download, follow, or support. Feature parity with the desktop is explicitly not the goal
and "catch the web up" is the framing this document exists to refuse.

## Goal 1 — the baseline

Measured before anything was changed, on loopback, desktop and phone viewports.

```
payload               539 KB over 11 requests
interactive           ~1.3 s (DOMContentLoaded, WASM reporting "schema v1")
sound                 one click — Start audio; the meter moves inside a second
above the fold        brand, Start audio, actions bar, hero pitch, the three
                      surfaces, the top of the graph
mobile                renders well; the actions bar scrolls sideways, so
                      "Join the mailing list" was invisible without a
                      horizontal scroll nobody performs
```

What already existed was most of a funnel: hero copy, a six-step tour with the mailing-list
panel gated on the golden moment, one-per-visit reporting, the three-surfaces list with its
no-invented-URLs rule, and a working WASM audio path. **The answer to "what can we throw
away" was: almost nothing — and the page was missing, rather than mis-building, four
things:**

1. **The design tokens.** Every colour was re-typed by eye — canvas `#14161a` against the
   desktop's `#0f1318`, accent `#6ee7b7` against `#57e3b4`, and audio cables drawn *white*
   against a cable pass that settled on mint. Exactly the drift
   `docs/design-tokens.json` was exported to prevent.
2. **The golden moment.** The graph was a picture. No pointer interaction on cables at all.
3. **The funnel's back half.** No support presence, no source link, no "different ways to
   see it", and the one conversion CTA hidden off-screen on phones.
4. **Legible cables.** Stroke widths lived in graph units, so a two-thousand-unit patch
   drew its cables two hairlines thick.

## Goal 2 — the funnel shell

- **`editor-web/tokens.css`**, generated by `tools/gen-web-tokens.mjs` from the desktop's
  own export and loaded after `style.css` so its `:root` wins. Values only — the knockout,
  the suppression rule, the LOD ladder stay in the design documents where they live.
  `web_tokens_match_desktop_export` sits in ctest beside the onboarding check, so a stale
  copy fails the gate instead of shipping.
- **The desktop's typeface** (Atkinson Hyperlegible Next, OFL, 112 KB, `font-display:
  swap`).
- **"One instrument. Different ways to see it."** — four cards from the canonical proofs,
  36 KB webp each or less, lazy-loaded so the first paint pays nothing.
- **"An open project"** — source (GitHub), the desktop's honest "no numbered release yet"
  with updates as the way to hear otherwise, and support said plainly: ears and words.
  No invented URLs, per `surfaces.js`'s standing rule.
- **On phones, Join moved into view** (`order: -1` under 700px) — conversion outranks
  "Save locally" on a screen where the bar scrolls sideways.
- The 400 KB claim was updated to the measured 650.

## Goal 3 — the golden moment

> normal graph → hover or lock a route → unrelated cables suppress →
> the focused route remains unchanged

Ported from `docs/graph-cable-system.md` onto the SVG graph view:

- **Hover** a cable and every route outside its family drops to 0.25 toward the canvas.
  The desktop mixes toward ground because a cord is six stacked draws and alpha lied
  there; an SVG cable is one stroke over the canvas, so opacity *is* that mix, at the
  same frozen figure.
- **The family is the output port** — two cables leaving one out are one signal fanning
  out and focus together, the desktop's port-family rule.
- **Click locks; click again, empty canvas, or Escape lets go.** Lock and hover render
  identically — no colour, border, glow, or width of its own, the desktop's rule kept
  verbatim.
- **The chosen route is never restyled.** Focus works because the noise leaves.
- **Nodes are never suppressed.**
- Each cable carries an invisible 24-px twin to take the pointer; the twins live below
  the nodes, so a cable cannot be grabbed through a node body — the desktop's hit-order
  arriving free with SVG paint order.
- Cables draw in **screen pixels** (`vector-effect: non-scaling-stroke`), which is the SVG
  spelling of the desktop's cords holding their weight at every zoom.

Verified end to end in the pane: `elementFromPoint` at a cable's own midpoint finds its
twin, a click locks `osc:out` and quiets the other seven, a real Escape releases it, and
the tour's node-dimming composes with route-quieting without either owning the other.

**"Want the whole instrument?"** appears exactly once, after a route is locked or thirty
seconds of listening — never before the visitor has done the thing the page exists for,
and never anywhere the full editor is not actually deployed.

## What the arc found in the frozen desktop

Putting the canonical shots side by side on a page found what six individual reviews had
not: **`rack.png` and `schematic.png` were two identical photographs of the Graph.** The
screenshot harness's `view` field only knew the Patch/Sandbox/Outline tab vocabulary, the
lens names matched nothing, and the miss was silent. The harness now speaks both
vocabularies and shouts on a name that is neither; all six canonical shots are retaken and
this time each was looked at. The web cards crop Face to its plate because the Face lens
ignores `fit` — recorded here rather than fixed, since framing a lens is desktop work and
the capture itself is honest.

## Known gaps, in order of next value

1. **No LICENSE file at the repo root.** The page says "developed in the open" instead of
   "free and open source" because no licence is declared anywhere. Naming one is a
   product decision; the funnel copy is ready to strengthen the day it exists.
2. **No crossing knockout on the web graph.** The demo patch keeps its crossings shallow,
   so nothing currently misreads — but the desktop grammar is only half present and a
   denser demo patch would expose it.
3. **The full editor is announced but not exported here.** `./editor/` 404s until
   `node tools/export-web.mjs --out editor-web/editor` has run against a deployment.
4. **The font ships as 112 KB of TTF.** A woff2 subset would roughly halve it; needs
   fontTools or equivalent.
5. **`verify-onboarding.mjs` knows nothing about the new sections.** The gallery images
   and project links have no staleness check the way the tour does.
